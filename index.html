Hi, I'm Carmen.
An engineer who likes to ask “what if?”

I'm a Biomedical Engineering graduate currently studying Biomedical Engineering and Medical Physics at TUM. I'm interested in the space where health, data, AI and visual communication meet — especially the part before the algorithm: the signal, the image, the label, the decision.

This portfolio is my playground for learning in public: building small things, asking better questions and making complex ideas easier to see.
